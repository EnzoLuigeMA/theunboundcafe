01 — March 10, 2014

The Beginning

DOX was born from Ivo's dream. We united our lives and refused the script others had written for us — the one that called our plan "impossible" and told us to seek "real jobs."

For those who come from the bottom, "no" is only the starting point.
02

12 Years of Resilience

Crises, a pandemic, a thousand reasons to stop. We did not merely survive — we matured. We learned to build with sweat, honor, and an unshakeable belief in the invisible.

03

International Roots

English was the key that opened global doors. Across oceans we learned the pillars of this house: the dignity of work, generosity, and the celebration of individual freedom.

04

A Shift in Paradigm

Every great transformation begins in the mind before it shows in the world. The Unbound House is that shift made physical — a monument to every battle won.

This place exists because we chose to build it when the world said we couldn't.
05

Your Expansion Hub

More than a café — a reference point for the English-speaking community. A place to interact, practice, and expand horizons. We bring the curation of our journeys into your cup.

06 — Our Legacy

You are exactly what you believe yourself to be. Do not conform. Do not settle for little.

The Unbound House exists for those who refuse to be bound.
